# MY MOVIES/SERIES

1. Interstellar is a 2014 science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan. It follows a group of astronauts led by Cooper who travel through a wormhole to find a new habitable planet as Earth faces environmental collapse. The movie explores themes of love, time, and survival, with stunning visuals and a powerful score by Hans Zimmer.

2. Parasite is a 2019 South Korean film directed by Bong Joon-ho. It tells the story of a poor family who cleverly infiltrates the lives of a wealthy household, leading to unexpected and tragic consequences. The film explores social inequality, class conflict, and greed, and won the Academy Award for Best Picture.

3. Queen of Tears is a 2024 South Korean drama series starring Kim Soo-hyun and Kim Ji-won. It follows the turbulent marriage between a wealthy heiress and her husband as they face personal struggles, family conflicts, and rediscover their love for each other. The series combines romance, comedy, and emotional drama, becoming one of the highest-rated K-dramas in history.

4. Brothers is a 2009 drama film directed by Jim Sheridan. It tells the story of two brothers, one a soldier presumed dead in Afghanistan and the other who grows close to his wife and children during his absence. When the soldier unexpectedly returns home, the family faces emotional turmoil, guilt, and tension as he struggles with trauma and betrayal.

5. *Ratatouille is a 2007 animated film produced by Pixar and directed by Brad Bird. It follows Remy, a rat with a passion for cooking, who teams up with a young chef named Linguini to create culinary masterpieces in a Paris restaurant. The film celebrates creativity, following one’s dreams, and the idea that anyone can cook, no matter where they come from.*
